Reinforcement Learning for Mobile Grasping with GARMI

GARMI coordinates its mobile base and right arm to approach a bowl on a kitchen counter. A policy trained with expert demonstrations and PPO adjusts the base and arm together, using robot state, object pose and a grasp reference. A fixed controller then closes the gripper and lifts the bowl. CRAM handles navigation, transport and placement in the complete GARMI apartment in Isaac Sim.

Grasping excerpt at original simulation playback speed. RL controls the approach; closing and lifting use a fixed controller.
